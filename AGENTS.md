# Cyanistic's Home

Cyanistic's Home is Cyan's personal website for sharing his work, interests, and technical writing through a distinctly personal Hyprland desktop and terminal experience.

## Product priorities

- Serve personal expression first and technically minded peers second.
- Keep the site credible to potential collaborators without sanding away its personality.
- Prefer an authored, focused personal site over a generic portfolio, a desktop simulator, or a broad publishing platform.

## Visual direction

- Preserve the spirit of a personal Hyprland workspace and terminal, not the current implementation of that metaphor.
- Upgrade the execution through deliberate hierarchy, coherence, restraint, and detail. More decoration is not automatically better design.
- Use the desktop metaphor as visual language. Keep content and navigation direct instead of simulating an operating system.
- For design choices, show concrete alternatives, recommend one, and iterate from Cyan's reactions. Do not require Cyan to translate abstract design vocabulary into a solution.

## Accessibility and completion

A major visual change is complete only when:

- the result looks deliberate on desktop and mobile;
- every interaction works with a keyboard and has a clear focus state;
- motion respects reduced-motion preferences without breaking the experience; and
- the result still feels personal rather than interchangeable with a generic portfolio.

Accessibility is a required outcome. Adapt the visual metaphor when necessary instead of making access the compromise.

## Voice and trust

- Keep the default voice personal, candid, playful, and technically exact. Let the topic control the balance: low-risk personal material can carry more humor, while consequential claims require restrained and precise language.
- Avoid generic marketing language. Preserve Cyan's own vocabulary and point of view.
- Verify claims about personal history, projects, security, features, availability, ownership, and licensing before publishing them. Ask or qualify the wording when the evidence is uncertain.
- Treat biography, personal positions, donation details, contact targets, sensitive information, and reputation-sensitive humor as high-cost material. Pause for Cyan's approval when a change could misstate or expose them.
- Treat free software as a principle, not a blanket GPL rule. Permissive licenses can suit libraries; copyleft licenses are generally preferred for standalone applications.

## Decision authority

Use case-by-case judgment rather than a fixed approval checklist. Proceed with routine implementation and polish that preserve the agreed direction. Ask Cyan before committing the site to a major identity or visual direction, or whenever uncertainty could cause a false claim, personal error, sensitive-data error, or reputational harm.
