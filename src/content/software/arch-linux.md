Arch is the only Linux distribution I've stuck with for longer than a week. It's minimal and user-friendly once you get over the initial hurdle of installing it. The difficulty of installation can usually be mitigated by using the [archinstall](https://wiki.archlinux.org/title/Archinstall) script.
