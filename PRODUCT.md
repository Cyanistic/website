# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are technically minded peers who want to understand Cyan and explore his projects, software choices, and technical writing. The site serves Cyan's personal expression first. Potential collaborators are a secondary audience; the site should remain credible without becoming impersonal.

## Product Purpose

Cyanistic's Home gives Cyan a focused place to publish his work, interests, and technical perspective. A successful visit quickly establishes who Cyan is and makes his projects, software, and writing easy to explore.

## Positioning

The site presents Cyan's real work and point of view through a personal Linux, Hyprland, and terminal-native identity. Real artifacts carry that identity; the interface helps visitors understand and explore them. Its value comes from authored character and technical substance rather than generic portfolio conventions.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a public website, move between personal and technical material, and follow links to real projects, repositories, and profiles. The site favors direct content and navigation over simulated operating-system behavior.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site publishes projects, software choices, technical writing, biography, and donation information.
- It remains a focused personal site rather than a desktop simulator or broad publishing platform.
- Free software is a principle, not a blanket GPL requirement. Permissive licenses can suit libraries; copyleft licenses are generally preferred for standalone applications.
- Public claims about Cyan, projects, security, features, availability, ownership, and licensing must be supported by evidence or qualified clearly.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Cyanistic's Home.
- The voice is personal, candid, playful, and technically exact. Humor can increase for low-risk personal material; consequential claims use restrained, precise language.
- Cyan's real Hyprland, Eww, and terminal setup is the primary visual reference. The website translates its design grammar into honest web functions instead of copying unrelated desktop widgets.
- Playfulness supports exploration and personal expression; it does not become the product or obstruct direct navigation.
- The site avoids generic marketing language and generic portfolio polish that removes Cyan's personality.

## Evidence on Hand

- Published biography and personal claims in `src/pages/about.astro`.
- Project and software descriptions in `src/content/projects/` and `src/content/software/`.
- Technical writing in `src/content/blog/`.
- External project, profile, and donation targets in the pages and content that render them.
- No verified testimonials, audience metrics, customer claims, or endorsements are available in the repository; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve Cyan's authorship and point of view.
2. Establish identity through real work; use summary copy to support the evidence rather than replace it.
3. Help visitors understand and explore without making the interface performative.
4. Make technical and personal claims only at the strength the evidence supports.
5. Improve design quality through hierarchy, coherence, restraint, and purposeful detail.
6. Keep accessibility part of the intended experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Major visual work must look deliberate on desktop and mobile, support keyboard interaction with clear focus states, and respect reduced-motion preferences without breaking hierarchy or comprehension. Adapt the visual metaphor when necessary rather than making accessibility the compromise.
